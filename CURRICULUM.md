# Curriculum Design

## Methods
- Communicative Language Teaching (CLT)
- Task-based learning
- Grammar in context
- Spaced repetition (SRS) and active recall
- Listening and extensive reading
- Shadowing and focused pronunciation practice
- Deliberate practice with error analysis and feedback

## Skill strands
1. Grammar: form, meaning, use, and sentence patterns.
2. Vocabulary: useful words and chunks, IPA, examples, and review prompts.
3. Reading: graded texts, comprehension, inference, vocabulary in context.
4. Listening: General American dialogues, gist, detail, connected speech.
5. Speaking: guided responses, role-play, fluency, interaction.
6. Writing: sentence building, messages, paragraphs, genre-specific writing.
7. Pronunciation: sounds, stress, rhythm, intonation, shadowing.

## Lesson cycle
Retrieval warm-up → input and explanation → guided practice → communicative task → feedback → homework → spaced review.

## Review intervals
Review new material after 1, 3, 7, 14, and 30 days. Adjust based on recall performance.

## Assessment policy
Record scores only for skills actually assessed. Do not infer listening, speaking, writing, or pronunciation scores from multiple-choice or reading tasks.
