# Lesson 002 — Questions, Answers, and Negatives

- Date: 2026-11-23
- Level: A1 (provisional)
- Accent: General American

## Goals
1. Form yes/no questions with am/is/are.
2. Form yes/no questions with do/does.
3. Give matching short answers.
4. Make basic negative sentences.

## Questions with be
Use am/is/are + subject + ...?
- You are happy. → Are you happy?
- She is a student. → Is she a student?
- I am late. → Am I late?

Short answers:
- Are you happy? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
- Is she a student? — Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
- Are they tired? — Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.

## Questions with do/does
Use do/does + subject + base verb?
- You live in Kazan. → Do you live in Kazan?
- She likes coffee. → Does she like coffee?
- He works here. → Does he work here?

Short answers:
- Do you work? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- Does he live here? — Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.

After does, use the base verb: Does she like...? (not Does she likes...?)

## Negative forms
With be:
- I am not tired.
- She isn't a teacher.
- We aren't tired.

With other verbs:
- I don't work.
- They don't like coffee.
- He doesn't live in America.

I/you/we/they → don't; he/she/it → doesn't. After doesn't, use the base verb.

## Error focus
1. He don't live... → He doesn't live... Use doesn't with he/she/it.
2. coffe → coffee. Remember final -ee.
