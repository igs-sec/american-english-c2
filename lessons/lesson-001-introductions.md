# Lesson 001 — Introducing Yourself

- Date: 2026-11-23
- Level: A1 (provisional)
- Focus: personal information, subject pronouns, and the verb **to be**
- Accent: General American
- Duration: 60 minutes

## Goals
By the end of the lesson, the learner can:
1. Introduce themselves using 3–5 simple sentences.
2. Choose `am`, `is`, or `are` in basic affirmative sentences.
3. Ask and answer simple questions about names, places, and feelings.

## 1. Core language

| Subject | Form of **be** | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I am Alex. |
| You | are | You are kind. |
| He / She / It | is | She is a student. |
| We / They | are | We are friends. |

Contractions in everyday American English:
- I am → I'm
- You are → You're
- He is → He's
- She is → She's
- We are → We're
- They are → They're

## 3. Model dialogue

A: Hi! What's your name?
B: My name is Alex. What's your name?
A: I'm Sam. Nice to meet you.
B: Nice to meet you, too.

Meaning:
A: Привет! Как тебя зовут?
B: Меня зовут Алекс. Как тебя зовут?
A: Я Сэм. Приятно познакомиться.
B: Мне тоже приятно познакомиться.

## 4. Grammar in context

- I am from Russia. → I'm from Russia.
- He is my friend. → He's my friend.
- They are students. → They're students.

Remember: use **am** with **I**, **is** with **he/she/it**, and **are** with **you/we/they**.

## 5. Practice

Complete with **am**, **is**, or **are**:
1. I ___ from Russia.
2. My name ___ Alex.
3. You ___ my friend.
4. We ___ students.
5. She ___ happy.

Translate into English:
6. Меня зовут Анна.
7. Я из России.
8. Мы друзья.

Write a short introduction (3–5 original sentences):
- My name is ...
- I am from ...
- I live in ...
- I am ...
- I like ...

## 6. Speaking and pronunciation
Read the model dialogue aloud twice. Then say your own introduction without reading. Pay attention to:
- **name** /neɪm/ — one clear syllable
- **live** /lɪv/ — short /ɪ/, not /iː/
- **I'm** /aɪm/ — one syllable

Pronunciation has not yet been scored; a text-only response cannot establish speaking accuracy.

## 7. Exit check
The lesson is complete when the learner can:
- choose all five forms correctly;
- translate at least 2 of 3 sentences correctly;
- produce a 3–5 sentence self-introduction.

## Homework
1. Review the eight vocabulary items.
2. Write five sentences about yourself.
3. Read your introduction aloud three times.
4. Be ready to answer: “What's your name?”, “Where are you from?”, and “Where do you live?”
