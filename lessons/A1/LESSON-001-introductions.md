# Lesson 001 — Introducing Yourself

- Level: A1 (provisional)
- Date: 2026-11-22
- Focus: greetings, names, personal information, and the verb `be`.
- Standard: General American English

## Goals
Greet someone, say your name, and produce basic sentences with `am`, `is`, and `are`.

## Core language
- Hello! — Привет!
- My name is … — Меня зовут …
- I am … years old. — Мне … лет.
- I live in … — Я живу в …
- I am from … — Я из …
- Nice to meet you. — Приятно познакомиться.

## Grammar: be
- I am
- You are
- He/She/It is
- We/They are

Examples:
- I am Alex.
- She is a student.
- They are happy.
- We are friends.

## Diagnostic work completed
- Basic vocabulary: 8/8.
- `am/is/are`: 4/4.
- Sentence translation: 4/4.
- Short reading comprehension: 5/5.
- Guided self-introduction: all 6 sentences grammatically correct.

## Learner's written work
1. My name is Ivan.
2. I am 39 years old.
3. I live in Kazan.
4. I am from Russia.
5. I like to watch films.
6. I want to learn English because I want to watch films in English.

Feedback: all six sentences are correct. Natural alternative: “I like watching films.” Another concise option: “I want to learn English because I enjoy watching films in English.”

## Skill coverage
Basic vocabulary, grammar, short-text reading, and a short guided writing task were sampled. Listening, speaking, and pronunciation remain unassessed.

## Homework
Prepare to answer these questions aloud or in writing:
- What's your name?
- How old are you?
- Where are you from?
- Where do you live?
- What do you like doing?
