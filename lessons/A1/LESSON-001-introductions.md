# Lesson 001 — Introducing Yourself

- Level: A1 (provisional)
- Date: 2026-11-22
- Focus: greetings, names, personal information, and basic sentence patterns.
- Standard: General American English

## Goals
Greet someone, introduce yourself, and produce basic sentences with `am`, `is`, `are`, `like`, and `want`.

## Core language
- Hello! — Привет!
- My name is … — Меня зовут …
- I am … years old. — Мне … лет.
- I live in … — Я живу в …
- I am from … — Я из …
- Nice to meet you. — Приятно познакомиться.

## Grammar: be
- I am
- You are
- He/She/It is
- We/They are

Examples:
- I am Alex.
- She is a student.
- They are happy.
- We are friends.

## Grammar: like and want
- `like + verb-ing`: I like watching films.
- `want + to + base verb`: I want to watch films in English.
- Note: `like + to + verb` is also grammatical (e.g. I like to watch films).
