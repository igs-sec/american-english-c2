# Lesson 001 — Introducing Yourself

- Level: A1 (provisional)
- Date: 2026-11-22
- Focus: greetings, names, personal information, and the verb `be`.
- Standard: General American English

## Goals
Greet someone, say your name, and produce basic sentences with `am`, `is`, and `are`.

## Core language
- Hello! — Привет!
- My name is … — Меня зовут …
- I am … years old. — Мне … лет.
- I live in … — Я живу в …
- I am from … — Я из …
- Nice to meet you. — Приятно познакомиться.

## Grammar: be
- I am
- You are
- He/She/It is
- We/They are

Examples:
- I am Alex.
- She is a student.
- They are happy.
- We are friends.

## Diagnostic work completed
- Basic vocabulary: 8/8.
- `am/is/are`: 4/4.
- Sentence translation: 4/4.
- Short reading comprehension: 5/5.

## Skill coverage
Vocabulary, basic grammar, and short-text reading were sampled. Independent writing, listening, speaking, and pronunciation remain unassessed.

## Homework
Write 5–6 simple sentences introducing yourself. Use at least three patterns: `My name is …`, `I am …`, `I live in …`, `I am from …`, `I like …`.
