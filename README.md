# American English: A0 to C2

A structured, interactive General American English learning system aligned with the CEFR, from beginner foundations to C2 proficiency.

## Student
- Starting placement: provisional A1 (Beginner)
- Daily study time: 60 minutes
- Goals: communication, work, travel, and education
- Explanation language: Russian initially, with gradual transition to English

## Learning approach
Communicative Language Teaching, task-based learning, spaced repetition, active recall, shadowing, grammar in context, listening, reading, and deliberate practice.

## Repository structure
- `STUDENT_PROFILE.md` — learner profile and assessed skills
- `PROGRESS.md` — progress and assessment record
- `ROADMAP.md` — CEFR progression plan
- `CURRICULUM.md` — curriculum and skill strands
- `levels/`, `lessons/`, `vocabulary/`, `grammar/`, `tests/`, `mistakes/`, `revision/`, `reports/` — learning materials

## Daily routine (60 minutes)
10 min vocabulary/review, 10 min grammar, 10 min listening, 10 min pronunciation/shadowing, 10 min reading, 10 min writing or speaking.

Advancement is assessment-based. Scores are recorded only for skills that have actually been assessed.
