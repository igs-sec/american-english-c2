# CEFR Roadmap: A1 to C2

Starting placement is provisional A1. A0 foundations remain available for targeted review. Advancement is assessment-based, not time-based.

| Stage | Main outcomes |
|---|---|
| A1 | Introductions, everyday phrases, basic questions, present simple, core vocabulary, short texts and dialogues. |
| A2 | Routine exchanges, past and future basics, descriptions, everyday needs, short messages. |
| B1 | Independent communication on familiar topics, connected speech, narratives, opinions, practical writing. |
| B2 | Fluency and spontaneity, complex texts, argumentation, professional and academic communication. |
| C1 | Flexible, precise language for demanding contexts; nuanced speaking and writing. |
| C2 | Effortless comprehension and highly precise, natural communication across complex contexts. |

Every stage develops grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, listening, speaking, and pronunciation. A stage is completed only after a balanced assessment; untested skills remain Not Assessed.
